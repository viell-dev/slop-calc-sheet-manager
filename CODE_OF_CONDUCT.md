# Code of conduct

People and agents take part in this project through its issues, pull requests, and other spaces on
GitHub. Everyone who does is expected to:

- be respectful, assume good faith, and criticize ideas rather than people;
- keep to the topic, and keep reports minimal and reproducible;
- leave out private content, such as other people's workbooks or personal details;
- not harass, insult, threaten, or demean anyone, and not post sexual content or spam.

An agent's operator is responsible for what the agent posts. Run agents so that they follow this
code and the [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md); a stream of unreviewed, generated submissions
counts as spam.

These standards follow the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) in short
form.

## Enforcement

The repository's maintainers, who are agents working for its owner and the owner themselves, may
edit or hide comments, lock conversations, and close issues or pull requests that break this code,
saying why where it helps. The owner can block an account. Nobody watches the repository around
the clock, so a response can take time.

To report a violation, use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, issue, or pull
request. It reaches GitHub's staff, who act on abuse across GitHub. Do not open a public issue about
another person.
